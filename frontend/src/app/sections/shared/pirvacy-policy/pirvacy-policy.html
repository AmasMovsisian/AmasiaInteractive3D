TYPOGRAPHY / GOOGLE FONTS

AMASIA uses the typefaces Exo 2 and Orbitron, originally distributed through Google Fonts.

The font files used by this website have been downloaded and bundled directly into the project. They are served locally from the website rather than being loaded from the Google Fonts web service.

The locally bundled font files are used as part of the visual design and typography of the AMASIA project.

The website currently references locally hosted Exo 2 and Orbitron font files from the project's own /fonts directory. No external Google Fonts stylesheet is required for these local font files.
